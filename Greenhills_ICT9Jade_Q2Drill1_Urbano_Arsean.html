Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Basic Rules

  • 6 players on the court per team at a time.
  • Best of 5 sets. The first 4 sets are played to 25 points. The 5th deciding set is played to 15 points.
  • 3 hits maximum per side before the ball must cross the net
  • Faults & boundary lines
